Top 3 Mini-Game Concepts

These short-session concepts focus on tight controls and clear goals in under five minutes.

1. Neon Courier

Deliver packages across rooftops before timer expires while dodging wind gusts.

2. Gravity Flip Lab

Switch room gravity between floor and ceiling to guide a magnetic core through spikes.

3. Dungeon Dish Duty

Prep potions and ingredients fast for impatient adventurers between wave battles.

Starter Tips for New Players

  1. Play one round on Normal speed to learn obstacle patterns.
  2. Focus on clean movement first before chasing combo multipliers.
  3. Take a short break between runs to keep reaction times sharp.
